    Printing Capabilities

    Three techniques.
    Endless possibilities.

    Whatever your run size, finish or budget — we use the right printing method for the job.

    01

    Flexo Printing

    A modern letterpress process using flexible relief plates. Ideal for high-volume runs on non-woven, paper and polypropylene with vivid, repeatable color.

    02

    Screen Printing

    Transfers a stencilled design through a mesh screen with ink and squeegee. Best for bold solid colors, durable finishes and short-to-medium runs.

    03

    Offset Printing

    Also called offset lithography. The industry standard for crisp, photographic-quality printing on paper bags and packaging at commercial scale.
